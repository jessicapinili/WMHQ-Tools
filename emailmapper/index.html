Stage 1 · The journey

Which journey are you building?

A journey is a set of emails that send automatically after someone does something. That action is the trigger. Pick the moment you're responding to, and the builder loads a proven starting map you can reshape.

Best on desktop. I recommend using this tool on a laptop or desktop, not your phone, so you can see the full map and every email side by side.
The point of the whole journey

If someone reads every email, what do they do at the end? One outcome. Not three.

Stage 2 · The emails

One email. One job.

Before any copy: decide what each email has to make happen, when it sends, who gets it, and the one thing you're asking them to do.

The one-job rule. If you need the word and to describe an email's job, it's two emails. If it has two CTAs, the reader picks neither.
Stage 3 · The map

Your journey, at a glance.

Read it from start to finish. Each email should hand the reader to the next one. If two neighbours do the same job, cut one.

Stage 4 · Export

Your journey, ready to write.

Every email in order. Your export includes the map, each email and a full journey audit, so you know what to fix before you write the copy.
