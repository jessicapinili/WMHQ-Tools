The only rule

One piece of video. Every day.

Yes, of course a carousel counts. But bonus points for video, and bonus points on top of that for talking head. Post to Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, or all three. Log more if you make more.

This is not a content quality challenge

It is a consistency experiment. Which means the mediocre days count, the phone-propped-in-the-car days count, and the days you posted nothing count too.

The data only works if the days are honest. Log the zeros. Do not go back later and tidy the chart.

Thirty circles, one per date. Each deepens as your count rises, so a heavy day looks different to a token one at a glance. By week three the shape of your actual rhythm is visible, including the gaps.
